Every experiment is walk-forward: each scored race uses only information from before it.

**Known limitation found by E4/E5.** Retirements are systematically under-predicted in 2026 (13–15% predicted against 18% observed per car). A pooled season-level prior (E5) reduces the bias in the mean but does not improve the race forecasts. A per-lap hazard model with exposure (planned) is the next candidate.

**Units.** Pace RMSE is in % of lap time, per race, over drivers with 8 or more clean laps in dry races. Intervals are 95% race-level bootstraps of the paired difference against the reference.
