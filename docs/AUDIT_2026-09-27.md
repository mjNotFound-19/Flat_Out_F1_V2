## 2. Event identity (fixed)

- **Finding.** The 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix (round 16, 2–4 October) is held at the **Sepang International Circuit, Malaysia**: 5.543 km, 56 laps, race start 07:00 UTC.
  - Source: <https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/bahrain>, checked 27 September.
  - FastF1 lists it with location `Kuala Lumpur` and country `Bahrain`.
- **Bug.** `pipeline.event_info()` substituted "the venue this event name used last year" (Sakhir). Every round 16 forecast (four runs on 26 September) therefore simulated Sakhir: 57 laps, with Sakhir's pit loss, tyre wear, safety-car rate and overtaking.
- **Fix** (commit `f1120a3`):
  - A sourced registry, `flatout/registry/circuits.json`, covers 26 circuits and maps every FastF1 location string explicitly.
  - `flatout/events.py` resolves each event. Unknown locations, a title/host-country mismatch without a sourced override, a changed feed, or a moved race time all raise `EventResolutionError`.
  - All 71 stored schedule rows resolve.
  - Tests: `tests/test_events.py`.
- **Old forecasts.** They were moved byte for byte, with hashes verified, to `predictions_v3/2026/R16/superseded/20260927_sakhir_venue_error/`, marked `invalid_for_event`.
- **Still wrong: the live site.** manasjha.online/f1 still shows a Sakhir-based round 16 forecast. Replacing it needs a publish, which this task does not do without an explicit release instruction.
- **Sepang has no race in the 2024–26 dataset.** The forecast therefore uses:
  - the official lap count
  - a base lap from the pooled median seconds per km (17.65 s/km over 25 circuits) × 5.543 km = 97.9 s
  - pooled all-circuit priors for pit loss, tyre wear, stops, safety-car rate and overtaking
  - driver pace sd × 1.25

  These are listed in the forecast metadata, and the status is `provisional`.
- **Not available.** There is no Sepang telemetry (FastF1 position data starts in 2018; Sepang's last race was 2017). The 3D circuit must show an honest fallback, not Sakhir geometry.

## 4. Leakage audit

| Component | Walk-forward safe? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Pace models in backtests | **Yes** | They are refitted per race with `cutoff=(year, rnd)`. Blend weight, residual sd and band multipliers come from a model calibrated only on races before the first backtested event. |
| Circuit priors | **Yes** | `circuits.build(analyses, cutoff=(year, rnd))`. |
| Form features | **Yes** | `features.form` uses only races before (year, rnd). |
| **Simulator parameters** | **No** | `models/sim_params.json` has 21 values, including `grid_blend`, `pass_thr` and `pace_sd_mult`. They were tuned by `calibrate --year 2026 --last-n 13` on 2026 rounds 3–15, where they scored 0.10723 in-sample. The "out-of-sample" 2026 backtests then re-scored exactly those races with those parameters. |
| Simulator parameters in the 2025 backtest | **No** | The 2025 races were scored with parameters tuned on later 2026 races. |
| Season weights, form half-lives, carry-over fractions | **Chosen by hand** | They were picked during development with knowledge of the results, so they are not out-of-sample. |

**Re-measured (nested, E3 in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`).** The post-quali model RPS is 0.1082 against the grid's 0.1204. The paired difference is −0.0122, 95% interval [−0.0190, −0.0064], in 12 of 13 races. The claim therefore survives out-of-sample; only its evidential status changes.

**Consequence (as found).** The reported 2026 figure (RPS 0.107 against the grid's 0.120, "11% better") and the 2025 figure (0.111 against 0.116) are **not clean out-of-sample results**. They are development numbers. Stage 2 re-measures them with nested walk-forward calibration: simulator parameters are tuned only on races before each evaluated race.

## 5. Reproducibility check

- A 6-race, 200k-simulation run of the 2025 backtest (rounds 3–8) reproduced the 4M-simulation per-race RPS to 3–4 decimal places (for example Suzuka 0.0671 at both). The Monte Carlo error at 4M is negligible next to the model differences.
- **Seeds.** The backtest seeds each race as `11 + index`, and forecasts seed each chunk as `2026 + 7919·i`. These are fixed, but they are not `SeedSequence` streams: changing the worker count changes the chunking and therefore the numbers. Stage 4 fixes this.
